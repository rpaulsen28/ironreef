Dive Consulting

Dive with a divemaster's eye

As a certified divemaster, I've led and supported divers in Caribbean waters and at freshwater dive sites across the US. Dive consulting puts that experience to work for your next trip, whether it's a reef vacation or a weekend at an inland site.

Saltwater

The Caribbean

Choosing islands and dive operators, matching reefs, walls and wrecks to your skill level, and planning dive days around the season and conditions.

Freshwater

US Freshwater Sites

Quarries, lakes and springs close to home: what to expect from cold water, thermoclines and limited visibility, and how to gear up for them.

01

Dive trip planning

Destination, operator and itinerary recommendations built around your certification level and goals.

02

Skills and readiness check-ins

Getting comfortable again before a trip, especially after time away from the water.

03

Gear guidance

What to buy, rent or bring for warm Caribbean water versus cold freshwater diving.

04

Group and club trip support

Planning help for dive clubs and groups heading out together, from logistics to dive-day briefings.

About Iron Reef

Strength on land, steadiness at sea

Iron Reef LLC was founded by Ryan Villanueva, a rodeo performer and competitor and a certified scuba divemaster. The bull and the anchor in the logo stand for both sides of the work.

After relocating from Colorado to the Kansas City area, Ryan rebuilt the business as Iron Reef LLC to bring rodeo and dive consulting together under one name.
